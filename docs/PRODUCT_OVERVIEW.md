# HeyNaj Flow Product Overview

HeyNaj Flow is an AI website assistant and business inquiry workspace.

Its core workflow:

1. A visitor asks a question on a website.
2. HeyNaj Flow answers using approved business Knowledge when relevant.
3. The conversation is preserved as an Inquiry.
4. Contact information can be captured depending on the Lead Capture setting.
5. The business receives useful context and eligible notifications.
6. The business reviews the Inquiry and follows up.

## Website experience

HeyNaj Flow supports Text Only, Standard Voice using browser/device speech features, and Premium Live Voice through compatible realtime voice providers.

## Business Knowledge

New Business Documents support TXT, MD, DOCX, and PDF with selectable text. Scanned or image-only PDFs are not currently searchable through OCR.

## Inquiry workspace

An Inquiry can include visitor-provided contact information, transcript, Conversation recap, Page URL, internal follow-up context, and important status.

The business remains responsible for final human follow-up.

## Notifications

Supported notification delivery can include Email and Web Push, depending on workspace configuration and provider availability.

## Question Review

Questions the assistant genuinely could not answer from available Knowledge can be surfaced for business review so missing information can be added.

## Widget customization

Businesses can configure parts of the website widget experience, including contact capture behavior and attention modes.

The widget retains **Powered by HeyNaj** branding.

## Installation

HeyNaj Flow uses a customer-owned Cloudflare workspace for the customer runtime.

Selected third-party services may still process information necessary for AI, voice, email, push, or related functionality.

## Free access

HeyNaj Flow is free to use. Optional coffee/community support is separate from product feature access. Third-party services may have their own limits, requirements, or charges.
